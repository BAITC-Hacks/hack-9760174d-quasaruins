# Project card artwork

Mode: built-in image generation. Created 23 September 2026 for Akim Lab. These are decorative AI-generated illustrations, not photographs or factual depictions of proposed construction. Live project names and budgets are rendered in HTML.

Art direction: one dominant project-specific object, readable on a small card, with an uncluttered light backdrop. No skyline, Bayterek, monuments, labels or generated text. Earlier skyline-heavy drafts were rejected and are not shipped. The city scene uses a separate architectural palette.

Keep cards opaque with a light background and use contain sizing so the silhouette remains visible. Source images are copied unchanged from the image-generation output.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a SQUARE, iconic project illustration for a small game card. This is a NEW design: one dominant, instantly recognizable subject, isolated in a studio on a plain pale warm-ivory background with a soft contact shadow. Close-up three-quarter isometric view, subject fills 75% of the image; generous clean edges for later HTML text overlays. High quality tactile matte 3D toy sculpture, simple bold silhouette, few details, pastel teal and sage with ivory and small amber accents. Readable at 140 pixels. No photographic cityscape, no skyline, no horizon, no distant scenery, no landmark buildings, no monuments, NO BAYTEREK, no spires, no decorative towers. No writing, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, card border or UI.
